Why the north edge?

It's a cool, sunny November day (high ~64°F), so we want sun, not shade. Our spot hugs the north tree line, ~23 ft west of the field's centre and ~65 ft in from the road. It gets full sun from 11:00 until about 4:05 PM, and it leaves the middle of the field open for every other family.

  • North trees never shade the north edge. Their shadows fall north, away from the field, all day.
  • The shade comes from the southwest. The woods across the road (bottom-left of the photo) are ~165 ft from our chairs. Their shadows reach us at about 4:05 PM, once the sun is low in the WSW. Drag the slider to watch it happen.
  • The SE and south tree lines shade their own edges at midday (~75 ft). The south end of the field is a narrow wedge that falls into shade first.
  • Chairs face south into the field, and into the sun. Bring hats and sunglasses: the sun is never higher than 37° today and sits low in the SW after 2 PM. The canopies shade the tables behind the chairs.
  • Wind: light WSW, 5–6 mph, coming across the open road side. It's light, but stake or weight the canopies anyway.
  • Bring layers. It's ~57°F at 11, 63–64°F from 2–4, and 56°F by 6 PM, and it's dark by 5:40.

The layout (1 unit = 1 ft)

  • 2 canopies, 10×10 ft, side by side. Their backs are 12 ft in from the north tree crowns.
  • 2 folding tables, 6×2.5 ft, one inside each canopy along the back edge.
  • 8 camp chairs in a gentle arc facing south, with a ~6-ft walkway in front of the canopies.

How this was built

  • Scale: the road is ~95 px across (perpendicular) in the 1439×1374 photo and is assumed to be 21 ft wide, giving 4.4 px/ft. (Your 4.3 is within the blur of the road edges.) The photo covers ~327 × 312 ft, and the field is ~195 ft E–W by ~230 ft N–S.
  • The field outline, the road (running ~23° west of north), the line of posts along the road, and the three goal frames were all traced from the photo. The goal sizes are estimates.
  • Trees are 40–62 ft tall. In summer imagery you can't see the trunks, so the tree edge is placed at the photo's crown edge. The SE edge is partly hidden in shadow in the photo, so it is the least certain.
